# College and school paper research notes (first pass, 2026-10-02)

## Covered (entries in publications/)
Stanford Daily, The Tech (MIT), Daily Collegian (Penn State), Orange and White /
Daily Beacon (Tennessee), Daily Princetonian, Daily Pennsylvanian, Daily Tar Heel,
Brown Daily Herald, Michigan Daily, Daily Northwestern, Daily Bruin, Red & Black
(Georgia), The Ubyssey (UBC), Bard Bulletin, TASIS Echo. Only Stanford, The Tech,
Collegian, Prince and DP are `researched`; the rest are `lead`.

## Tool caveats
- WebFetch is a summarizer and was sometimes wrong (it first gave two
  different NYT/Tribune timelines for Stanford; re-fetching with a request for
  verbatim sentences fixed it). Re-ask for verbatim quotes on anything important.
- 403 or bot-check on: Brown Daily Herald, Brown Digital Repository, Michigan
  Bentley archive, Illinois IDNC, Cornell alumni, Columbia library blog,
  Yale Daily News. Brown and Michigan archive years in entries are from
  search-result excerpts and are flagged as such.
- wpultimo.com pages ("Cracking the Code ... Crossword Clue") are AI-generated
  spam with invented history (Yale "New Haven Collegians Crossword", Illinois
  "University crossword"). Ignored; do not cite.

## Archives confirmed on an opened page (no crossword evidence yet)
- Yale Daily News Historical Archive, https://ydnhistorical.library.yale.edu/ ,
  1878–present, free (https://web.library.yale.edu/digital-collections/yale-daily-news-historical-archive).
- Cornell Daily Sun, https://cdsun.library.cornell.edu/ , 1880–present (first 50 years
  funded by Keith Johnson '56, https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2007/05/keith-johnson-56-funds-digitization-cornell-daily-sun).
- Columbia Spectator archive, 1877–present as of 2012 (infodocket).
- Daily Texan, Texas ScholarWorks, 1900–2019, free, issue-level search, poor
  pre-1960 OCR (https://guides.lib.utexas.edu/dailytexan/search).
- Daily Illini via Illinois Digital Newspaper Collections: the "1874–2010" figure is
  only from a search excerpt; page blocked.
These were not made entries because I found no sourced evidence they ran crosswords.
Full-text search of each archive for "crossword" / "cross-word" is the obvious next step
and would give first-appearance dates cheaply.

## Leads for a next pass
- Stanford's 2023 feature is the model: look for similar "history of our crossword"
  pieces in each paper's own site (search "[paper] crossword history" on their domain).
- Mike Shenk / Collegian: Will Shortz's remark that college crosswords are usually
  weekly or less suggests many papers ran one; constructor bios (xwordinfo,
  Wikipedia) are a good source of "wrote the crossword for X, years".
- Campus Crosswords (2012, Milo Beckman, Peter Broda, Caleb Madison): nothing found;
  not given an entry.
- 1924–25 craze: UT Orange and White ran a Knoxville News puzzle 7 Feb 1925; the Daily
  Tar Heel may have remarked on the craze in Dec 1924 (DigitalNC page, text not read).
  Free OCR'd archives (DigitalNC, Penn State, Princeton, Stanford, IDNC) make early
  adoption dates findable for many other papers.
- Chronicling America / state digital newspaper programs host some college papers.
- High school papers: no good sources found in this pass; Bard Bulletin and TASIS
  Echo are the only ones, both via the puzzle files rather than any web source.
- Syndication services for student papers (College Crossword / Campus Crosswords,
  U-Wire, Tribune Media Services college feeds) were not researched.
- Current-day "games desk" pages (Princetonian, DP, Michigan, Northwestern, Brown)
  show many papers now run student-made minis; staff lists give editors for 2020s.
