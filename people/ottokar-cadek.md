**Evidence.** The book identifies him: its note on No. 141 says "Mr. Cadek is quite a
figure in that field of music where the string quartette raises its austere voice."
Born in Chattanooga, he was chosen in 1919 as first violin of the New York String
Quartet (sponsored by Ralph Pulitzer) and made his New York debut in 1922. He married
Sara Hitchcock in 1924, the year of this "Mr. and Mrs." byline. He died on stage at
Interlochen in 1956. The University of Alabama holds the Ottokar T. Cadek papers,
middle initial included.

**Open.** His papers include diaries for 1897–1956, which might mention the puzzles. Who
was "Mrs." in the byline: Sara Hitchcock Cadek? No. 134's note praises "this winged
masterpiece": look for other Cadek puzzles in 1924–25 newspapers and the later series.
