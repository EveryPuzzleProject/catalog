The full write-up is the blog post
[The Harvard student behind two of Judge's most creative crosswords](https://blitz.xwordapp.com/blog/pelham.html)
(shared by link only).

**Evidence.** The Harvard Business School yearbook for 1924–25 lists Alfred Montgomery
Pelham (Michigan A.B. '22) at 378 Harvard Street, the address printed with both puzzles
(found by Ben Zimmer). Harvard's 1970 alumni directory and his Detroit Free Press
obituary put him at the business school 1922–25. Both puzzles draw pictures with shaded
squares, which no other Judge constructor did in 1924–25, and both clue AN as "Just
one." A 1977 Free Press profile says he spent his life inventing word games. No record
found so far names a crossword.

**Open.** Watch for "Pelham" bylines in other 1920s sources: newspapers, the Black
press, campus magazines, the 1924–25 crossword books. Michigan papers of the 1950s–60s
have been searched without result.
