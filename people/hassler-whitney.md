**Evidence.** The name is close to unique (he was named for his ancestor Ferdinand
Hassler). He grew up in New York and was 17 in 1924. The puzzle is titled "Boola Boola",
Yale's fight song, and the book's note calls it a puzzle "from New Haven" that "is
undoubtedly meant for freshmen". Whitney went to Yale (Ph.B. 1928). Nothing found yet
says he made crosswords.
