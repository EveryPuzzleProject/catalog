A Los Angeles daily (no Sunday edition), titled *Illustrated Daily News* from
1923 to 1936 and *Daily News* from 1936 to 1954, according to the Library of
Congress directory records in `census/loc-titles.csv` (LCCNs sn92055173,
sn82014920, sn87062121). It isn't the same paper as the *Daily News* that
starts in 1981 in the same records (sn81000823).

Alex DeJarnatt found it running a crossword by 1941.
