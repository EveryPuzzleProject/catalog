Editor's note in the 11 January 2012 issue
(https://thetech.com/2012/01/11/xwordnote-v131-n60): starting with that issue
The Tech runs Newsday crosswords from Creators Syndicate. Because the paper
came out twice a week and Newsday is a daily, the editors could choose which
day's puzzle to use; the first they chose was a Saturday (the hardest of the
week). The note does not say what ran before. Archive coverage and the
Internet Archive link are from the MIT Libraries guide
(https://libguides.mit.edu/MITPeople/other).

Open questions: when crosswords first appeared in The Tech; what ran before
2012 (the issues are on the Internet Archive and the site, so this can be
read directly); whether the Newsday feature continued after 2012.
