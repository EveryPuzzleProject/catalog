Running a Sunday "Cross-Word Puzzle Corner" by October 31, 1920, with a puzzle sent in
by Edgar W. Smith of Brooklyn. The same heading appears in the Omaha Daily News on July
11, 1920 ([omaha-daily-news.md](omaha-daily-news.md)), which suggests a shared,
syndicated source for both, though that isn't established. T Campbell's 1921 post
mentions a syndicated puzzle in this paper too
([1921: The Year in Crosswords](https://tcampbell.substack.com/p/1921-the-year-in-crosswords),
read through a search summary).

In 1925 the Courier carried Judge's "Cross Word Funnies" (see
[judge.md](judge.md)).

The paper merged with the Buffalo Express in 1926 to become the Courier-Express, and
Newspapers.com files the earlier Courier under that later name (date of the merger to
check).

Open questions: when the Corner began, and its source; whether it is the same feature
as Omaha's; whether it printed the World's puzzles before 1920; Edgar W. Smith (see
[people/leads.md](../people/leads.md)).
