The Free Press was running a weekly Sunday crossword by the end of 1919: the January 4,
1920 puzzle prints the solution to the previous week's. The heading ("Here's Another")
suggests it wasn't new then.

Whose puzzle it was is not known. The clue numbering (start–end square, "1-4") is the
New York World's style, and other papers were reprinting the World's FUN puzzles then
(see [first-runs.md](../first-runs.md)). But T Campbell finds the World with no new
crosswords for about two months in early 1920, which would make a new puzzle on
January 4 either one of the last World puzzles before the gap, or not the World's.

Open questions: the first Free Press crossword; whether these are World puzzles
(compare with the World's, or with other FUN partners' of the same weeks); a byline or
copyright line anywhere on the page; how long the series ran.
