Volopedia (UT Libraries) says the Orange and White "first published a crossword
puzzle on February 7, 1925", and that the puzzle came from the Knoxville News
(https://volopedia.lib.utk.edu/entries/first-crossword-puzzle-in-the-student-newspaper).
So this was a borrowed, locally syndicated puzzle, not one made in-house.
The paper first appeared in 1906 as a weekly and became the Daily Beacon in
1965 (https://volopedia.lib.utk.edu/?p=3037).
