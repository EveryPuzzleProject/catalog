Four copies of FUN's crossword so far, 1916-08-06, 1917-02-04, 03-04 and 12-02 (Newspapers.com page, Alex DeJarnatt, 2026-10-27/29; newyorkworld printings.tsv).
Newspapers.com files the pages as "The Times"; the masthead is legible only on 1917-03-04,
so the others are presumed to be the same paper.

Miss Bertha L. Botjer's puzzle ran here on 1917-02-04, the day the Pittsburgh Press
printed its solution, so the Press ran it about a week earlier (inferred; see
[pittsburgh-press.md](pittsburgh-press.md)) and this paper a week behind.

pages is the same paper; any FUN credit beyond the heading.
