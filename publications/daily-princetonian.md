Today the Prince has a puzzles team (head and associate editors, senior and
contributing constructors) and hosts crosswords at crossword.dailyprincetonian.com
(https://www.dailyprincetonian.com/section/puzzles). Archive details from the
University Archives post
(https://universityarchives.princeton.edu/2012/05/the-daily-princetonian-is-digitized-and-keyword-searchable).

Open questions: when crosswords first ran in the Prince, and whether they were
syndicated or in-house in earlier decades. The archive is full-text searchable,
so a search for "crossword" would likely date the first appearance.
