"The Cross-Word Puzzle Corner", in the paper's Sunday magazine, printed readers'
puzzles: "Lottie M. Arnold, Council Bluffs, Ia., is the author of this Cross-Word
Puzzle. The solution will be given in next Sunday's Omaha Daily News Magazine." Her grid
is built of separate sections with their own clue lists (Top, Left Side, Bottom, Right
Side) around a central Horizontal/Vertical part, numbered by start and end square
(1-4 A narrative; vertical 2-9 An indefinite article). From a clipping Alex DeJarnatt
shared on 2026-10-27; **its date is still to be recorded**, which is why there is no
sighting yet.

A second clipping, headed "Cross-Word Puzzle Corner" ("Here is a cross word square, by
Edgar W. Smith of Brooklyn, N. Y., which will give the lovers of long words a thrill"),
may be from the same feature; its paper and date are also to be recorded (see
[first-runs.md](../first-runs.md)).

Open questions: the dates of both clippings; when the Corner began and ended; whether
the Edgar W. Smith puzzle is this paper's; whether the Corner took puzzles from readers
everywhere or reprinted another paper's.
