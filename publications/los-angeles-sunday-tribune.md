Ran FUN's crossword as "Fun's Cross-Word Puzzle", with the same rebus ("Can You Read This
Letter?") and the same "complete list of clues" wording as the other FUN partners. Eight
copies so far, 1916-08-06 to 1917-12-02 (Newspapers.com page, Alex DeJarnatt, 2026-10-27/29; newyorkworld printings.tsv); Newspapers.com
files them as "Morning Tribune" and one as "Rural World", but the mastheads read The Los
Angeles Sunday Tribune.

Its copies credit the constructor by name and address, like the World's other partners:
Philip Malagrino (1916-12-03), Mae B. Drugan (1917-02-18), May E. Nugent (03-04), H. H.
Johnson (04-01).

**It ran well behind the others.** The Santa Claus puzzle came out on 1917-02-25. H. H.
Johnson's puzzle ran here on 1917-04-01, six weeks after Tacoma (02-18) and seven after
Pittsburgh (02-11, inferred from the solution Pittsburgh printed 02-18). Printings, not
World dates: see [first-runs.md](../first-runs.md).

Open questions: why the 1917-04-01 page prints the solution of May E. Nugent's puzzle (run
here 03-04) as "last week's", four weeks on; the start and end of its run; its
FUN credit, if any, beyond the heading.
