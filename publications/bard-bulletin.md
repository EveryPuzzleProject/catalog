Four undated crosswords by Caleb Madison titled "Bard Bulletin #1", "#2 an ego
boOSTER", "#3" and "A Swift Response" sit in the xword-ocr "bwh" file set
(see kb/briefs/bard-bulletin.md in gxd-contrib). Madison went to Bard High
School Early College, had a first NYT puzzle at 15 and entered Yale in fall
2011 (https://www.yalealumnimagazine.com/articles/4836-caleb-madison), so the
Bulletin is most likely that school's paper, c. 2008–2011. That identification
is an inference, not confirmed.

Open questions: confirm the paper, issue dates, and whether any scans of the
Bulletin exist.
