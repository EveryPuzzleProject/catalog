Reprinted the New York World's Sunday FUN section, crossword included, from 1915 or
earlier: a second source for the World's puzzles. Alex DeJarnatt independently found a
Minneapolis paper carrying FUN puzzles (2026-10); whether it's this one, and the dates
seen, are still to be recorded here.

In mid-March 1920, when the World's FUN section had gone two months without new
crosswords, the Journal and the Pittsburgh Press began running "Jerry King's Cross-Word
Puzzle" instead (see [pittsburgh-press.md](pittsburgh-press.md)).

Open questions: the first and last FUN reprints; how far behind the World they ran;
whether the reprints carry the World's credit; whether the Journal ever made its own.
