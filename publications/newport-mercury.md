A weekly that ran the Western Newspaper Union (WNU) crossword for about 14
months, January 1925 to March 1926. It is the catalog's first entry for a
ready-print weekly series, and WNU appears nowhere in the E&P Syndicate
Directory extraction (`census/ep-crossword-features.csv`). See
[research-notes-weekly-syndication.md](../research-notes-weekly-syndication.md).

The run, as found so far:

- No. 1, "Stepping Stones", on 1925-01-10 started the series, and is probably
  the Mercury's first crossword (unchecked: look at December 1924 issues).
- Nos. 1–7 ran every Saturday, 1925-01-10 to 02-21, each printing the
  previous puzzle's solution.
- No. 10 is on 1925-04-25. Weekly from No. 7 it would have run 1925-03-14,
  so at least six weeks were skipped; Nos. 8 and 9 fall somewhere between
  1925-02-28 and 04-18 and haven't been located.
- No. 12 is the last numbered puzzle (date not recorded). After it the
  puzzles carry no number or section title, so they are hard to find by
  search.
- The last puzzle is 1926-03-13; on 1926-03-20 the paper printed its
  solution and no new puzzle, so the series ended there.

The series is WNU's from No. 1: the numbering is continuous, every issue
answers the one before, and the WNU credit appears from No. 7 (Nos. 1 and 5
say only "(copyright 1924)", so the series began in 1924).

How it was found: searching the Northeast Collection for "cross-word puzzle
no.", then "western newspaper union", then "horizontal" and "vertical" with
"solution" for the unnamed puzzles. The 1926-03-13 puzzle didn't show up in
any search, so a missing hit is not a missing puzzle: an exact count needs a
page-by-page check, best done by noting the page the puzzle usually ran on
(ready-print pages came preprinted, so it may be the same page every week).

If it ran every week the run is 62 puzzles; with the six skipped weeks known,
the Mercury printed at most 56.

Open questions:
- Where do Nos. 8, 9, 11 and 12 fall, and on what page did the puzzle run?
- Was 1925-01-10 the Mercury's first crossword?
- How many puzzles did WNU issue? A second weekly carrying the series would
  show whether the Mercury's gaps were its own or WNU's. Searching other
  papers for the titles "Stepping Stones" and "The Merry Go Round" near
  "cross-word" should find the series and each paper's lag.
