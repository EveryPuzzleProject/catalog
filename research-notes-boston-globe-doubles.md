# Boston Globe Magazine double puzzle issues (2026-10-06)

Some Sundays the Globe Magazine printed two full-size crosswords on facing
pages, headed "The Globe Puzzle / Harder" and "The Globe Puzzle / Easier",
with "extra puzzles" on the cover. The Globe's website carried only the
Harder one (all 7 checked); the syndicated copies in gxd's 2015 files are
mostly the Easier one. Found by Alex DeJarnatt on Newspapers.com, searching
"Globe Puzzle" and "Harder" and "Themeless Challenger" (the text search
misses some issues). Entry: [publications/boston-globe.md](publications/boston-globe.md).
