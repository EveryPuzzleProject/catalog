The Associated Press's features arm sold a daily crossword for seventy years, made by
one family: Van Cleft Cooper ("J." in the 1930s, "G." by 1948, which may be OCR), then
Nina Cooper, then Doug Cooper. Chet Currier (Chester Currier, 1945–2007), an AP and later
Bloomberg financial columnist, made its Sunday puzzle from about 1979
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_Currier)).

Open questions: the Coopers' relationships and full names (a [people/](../people/)
entry); whether the AP crossword continued after 2000; copyright lines as printed.
