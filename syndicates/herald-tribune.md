The Herald Tribune's puzzle was syndicated from the first directory (1924). From 1963
it is listed under Publishers Newspaper Syndicate, still credited to Herald Tribune
staff; the Herald Tribune itself closed in 1966. Last listed in 1970.

Open questions: who edited it; what it was called in print; whether the Publishers
puzzle after 1966 was a backlog or new work.
