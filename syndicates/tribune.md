The Chicago Tribune and the New York Daily News (both owned by the Tribune company)
sold their features together. The syndicate's crossword is in every E&P directory from
1934 to 2000 under one of its names, and before that, as the Chicago Tribune Syndicate,
in 1925. The Stanford Daily ran "Tribune Media Services crosswords" for about eleven
years in the early 2000s ([stanford-daily.md](../publications/stanford-daily.md)).

Herb Ettenson edited the daily and Sunday puzzles from 1975 to at least 1996. Wayne
Robert Williams is named from 1999 and was the Chicago Tribune's crossword editor from
1997; TMS stopped distributing his puzzles in 2009 (see
[wayne-robert-williams.md](wayne-robert-williams.md)). Who edited the TMS puzzles
after that is not recorded here yet.

From 2000 TMS also sold the Los Angeles Times crossword: Tribune bought Times Mirror
that year and the Los Angeles Times Syndicate became part of TMS (see
[los-angeles-times-syndicate.md](los-angeles-times-syndicate.md)).

In 2013 the newspaper-syndication side of Tribune Media Services became Tribune
Content Agency (per Wikipedia, which also says TCA offers eight crosswords); the rest
of TMS went to Gracenote in 2014. The exact date wants a primary source.

Open questions: which TMS/TCA crosswords exist now and who edits them; copyright
lines as printed for each name ("© Chicago Tribune–N.Y. News Syndicate", "© Tribune
Media Services"?); whether the Chicago Tribune's own puzzle and the syndicated one
were ever the same (see [chicago-tribune.md](../publications/chicago-tribune.md)).
