General Features sold Margaret Farrar's daily from 1954, while she was editing the New
York Times puzzle. The LA Times printed it from at least 1962. The Los Angeles Times
Syndicate took GFC over in 1973–74 and kept selling the Farrar puzzle, now as the
"Los Angeles Times Crossword". So the syndicated "LA Times crossword" of the 1970s
began as GFC's, not the Times's own (details in
[los-angeles-times.md](../publications/los-angeles-times.md)). From 1984 E&P credits
it to "Staff".

Open questions: who edited the LA Times puzzle from 1984 (E&P says "Staff"); how the
syndicate's puzzle relates to the one the Times printed; what the Los
Angeles Times crossword's syndication looked like inside TMS.

Tribune bought Times Mirror, the LA Times's owner, in 2000 (merger completed June 12,
2000), and the Los Angeles Times Syndicate became a division of Tribune Media Services
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times_Syndicate); Tribune's 2000
SEC filings). So from 2000 the LA Times crossword and the old Chicago Tribune–New York
News crosswords were sold by the same company; see [tribune.md](tribune.md).
