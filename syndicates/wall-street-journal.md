Alex's list has the Journal as a paper that syndicates its own puzzle, like the New York
Times. That isn't confirmed: the Journal is in none of E&P's directories from 1924 to
2000, and a search (2026-10-27) found nothing on its crossword being sold to other
papers. Its puzzles are edited by Mike Shenk and collected in books.

Open questions: does the Journal's crossword run anywhere else, in print or online?
When did the Journal start a crossword (a weekend puzzle first, then daily)?
