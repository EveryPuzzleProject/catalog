Not syndicates: the software that serves a publication's puzzles online. They matter for
the archive because they decide what a puzzle looks like online and what survives (a
paper's online archive can vanish when it changes platform: the Boston Globe's old
crossword pages now return 404, see [boston-globe.md](../publications/boston-globe.md)).

- **Amuse Labs** makes PuzzleMe, a platform for crosswords and other games, used by
  publishers including the Washington Post and the LA Times (its own listing).
- **Arkadium** is on Alex's list as an online player; nothing about its crossword
  work was found yet. Does it host other papers' puzzles, sell its own, or both?

Open questions: which publications use which platform, and from when; whether a
platform's puzzles are the paper's own or a syndicate's (a paper's PuzzleMe page may
serve a syndicated puzzle); Puzzmo (the Boston Globe moved its games there in 2026) is
the same kind of thing.
