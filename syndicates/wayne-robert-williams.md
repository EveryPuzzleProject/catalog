Alex's list has Williams among the editors who ran their own syndication. Per the
Inquirer (2010), that came late: he edited the Tribune Media Services crosswords (E&P
names him for 1999–2000; a publisher's bio says he was the Chicago Tribune's crossword
editor from 1997), TMS stopped distributing his puzzles in 2009, and about six months
later he began syndicating them himself. The Inquirer had run his puzzles for years
and took them back ("reinstated").

So a "Wayne Robert Williams" byline before 2009 is a TMS puzzle; after about 2010 it is
his own syndication. The copyright lines should show the change.

He is also known for Dell Champion puzzle magazines and many puzzle books.

syndication, and for how long; the copyright lines before and after 2009.
