A subscription service founded in 2018 by Tracy Bennett and Laura Braunstein, funded by
Kickstarter (more than $30,000 from 800 backers), publishing crosswords by women and
nonbinary constructors: two a month at first, then three; about 1,500 subscribers and
50 constructors by 2021 (American Libraries, 2021). A book, *Inkubator Crosswords*,
collects 100 of them.

Direct to the solver: no newspaper carries it, so its puzzles survive only in the
service's own files, solvers' downloads and the book.

Open questions: whether it's still running; how many puzzles in all.
