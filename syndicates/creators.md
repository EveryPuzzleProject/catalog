Creators sells Stan Newman's Newsday crossword, listed from 1991 alongside Newman's own
[American Crossword Federation](american-crossword-federation.md), which listed the
same puzzle. How the two shared it (one for daily papers, one for others? one sold
the other's?) isn't clear from the directories. The Tech (MIT) runs the Newsday
crossword through Creators ([the-tech-mit.md](../publications/the-tech-mit.md)).

Open questions: how Creators and the ACF divided the Newsday puzzle; whether Creators
still sells it; copyright lines as printed ("© Stanley Newman, distributed by Creators
Syndicate"?).
