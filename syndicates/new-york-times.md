The Times sells its own puzzle to other papers, under a string of names for its
syndication arm. It first appears in E&P in 1972, under Will Weng's editorship, though
the directory names an editor only from 1978 (Maleska). The Stanford Daily carried it
until 2003 ([stanford-daily.md](../publications/stanford-daily.md)).

A syndicated Times puzzle runs some time after the Times printed it, so a copy in
another paper carries a later date than the original. How long the delay was, and
whether it changed, is not recorded here yet.

Open questions: was the Times puzzle syndicated before 1972; the delay over the years;
copyright lines as printed ("© The New York Times", "Edited by Will Shortz"); whether
the Times still syndicates to print papers.
