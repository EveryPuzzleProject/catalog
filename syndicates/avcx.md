Ben Tausig's puzzles ran in alt-weeklies across the country (the Village Voice, the
Chicago Reader, the San Francisco Bay Guardian among them) and, for about six years to
2012, as the crossword of The Onion's A.V. Club section. When that ended, a Kickstarter
raised more than $26,000 and the puzzle became the American Values Club Crossword, sent
to subscribers weekly by email: about 3,000 subscribers in 2014, then the largest
subscription puzzle (Tablet, 2014–15).

So the AVCX has a print history first, in alt-weeklies, before it went direct to
solvers. How the alt-weekly puzzle was distributed (sold by Tausig to each paper, or
through a service) is not known. Alex remembered an earlier name, "Ink Well"; not
confirmed by the sources found so far.
