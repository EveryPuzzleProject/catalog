The first rival to the World's puzzle that we know of. In early 1920, when the World's
FUN section had gone about two months without new crosswords, Walter A. Walker
registered a copyright through the Pittsburg Press for "Jerry King's Crossword Puzzle"
(Jerry King is probably a pseudonym). From mid-March the Press and the Minneapolis
Journal ran it in place of the World's, and the Vancouver Sun took it too (Campbell,
1920). It was still running in 1921, "more reliable than the World but not without its
eccentricities" (Campbell, 1921).

The Press's copy of 1920-03-14 promises the solution next Sunday and has no earlier
solution: probably the first ([pittsburgh-press.md](../publications/pittsburgh-press.md)).

Open questions: who Walter A. Walker was, and whether he was "Jerry King"; how many
puzzles and until when; every paper that carried it; the copyright line as printed.
