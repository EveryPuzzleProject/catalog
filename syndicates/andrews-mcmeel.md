The largest independent newspaper syndicate in the US (its own description), in Kansas
City. This is where the USA Today crossword comes from: Andrews McMeel Syndication
distributes it, and in December 2019 brought its editing in house, with Erik Agard as
editor. The copyright line on the USA Today puzzle names AMU because AMU owns or
distributes it, not USA Today.

**Universal Crossword** began as Timothy Parker's puzzle; by early 1997 Universal Press
Syndicate was selling it as "The Universal Crossword", with entertainment and sports
editions by 1999. Its copyright belongs to the syndicate, not the editor. In 2016,
after FiveThirtyEight reported puzzles in Parker's crosswords that repeated earlier
published ones, Universal Uclick confirmed some of the allegations and Parker took a
leave. David Steinberg later became Universal's editor. The Boston Globe ran Universal
as its online weekday crossword from at least 2015 to 2024
([boston-globe.md](../publications/boston-globe.md)).

**The Puzzle Society** is AMU's, not an independent service: Parker founded it in 1999
with Uclick, Universal Press's digital arm.

**United Media.** Since June 2011 AMU (then Universal Uclick) has syndicated United
Media's features for Scripps, which kept the copyrights. A crossword from that line may
be sold by AMU while its copyright names someone else.
