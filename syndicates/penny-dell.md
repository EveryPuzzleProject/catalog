The puzzle-magazine side of the landscape, which mostly sells to solvers rather than to
papers.

**Penny Press** was founded in 1973 in Stamford, Connecticut, by Bill (William E.) and
Penny Kanter, and is named for Penny. It bought **Dell Magazines** (founded 1921 by
George T. Delacorte) in March 1996; together they are Penny Dell, now in Norwalk,
Connecticut. Peter Kanter, their son, is Penny Press's president.

**The clubs and Three Across.** Alex remembered the Uptown Puzzle Club as acquired by
Penny Dell. What the sources show is an arrangement, not a purchase: from 2001, "by
arrangement with Three Across", Penny Publications has supplied "New York Times style"
crosswords to the Crosswords Club, the Large-Print Crosswords Club and the Uptown
Puzzle Club. Three Across, LLC owns the "Crosswords Club" trademark (filed 2000-04-13,
serial 76025377). It is registered at 6 Prowitt Street, Norwalk, Penny Publications'
address, with Peter Kanter as its member. So Three Across is a Kanter company beside
Penny, not an outside one. What it was before 2001, and whether the clubs
existed before Three Across (and under whom), is not known.

Dell puzzles also reached newspapers: Copley News Service sold a "Crossword Puzzle
(Dell)" in 1972–74 and from "Dell Books" in 1987–88 (E&P).
