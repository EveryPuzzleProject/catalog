Stan Newman's own outfit, listing the Newsday crossword he edited from 1990, a year
before Creators Syndicate lists it too (see [creators.md](creators.md)). One of the
editors in the 1980s–2000s who sold their own puzzle, with Merl Reagle and later Wayne
Robert Williams.
