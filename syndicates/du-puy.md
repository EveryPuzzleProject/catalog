All that is known is this one copyright line. The Minneapolis Journal's puzzle page
carries "(Copyright, 1914, by the Du Puy Syndicate.)" under the banner from 1914-02-15
through 04-12 (World Nos. 9 to 17), and "(Copyright, 1914, by the Press Publishing Co.)"
from 04-19, the World's own publisher (Alex DeJarnatt, Newspapers.com, 2026-10-27/29;
newyorkworld printings.tsv). So the Du Puy line preceded the World's own.

The line is on the page's banner, so it may cover the whole puzzle page and not the
crossword alone. Nothing else about the outfit is known here.

Open questions: who ran it; whether it sold the World's FUN page to other papers or only
packaged it for the Journal; whether the 1914-02-01 and 02-08 pages (no line seen) fall
before it; whether other partners printed the line. See
[new-york-world.md](new-york-world.md).
